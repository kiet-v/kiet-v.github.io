As a part of MIT 2.008 (Design and Manufacturing II), we learn about manufacturing processes, and considerations (cost, reliability, quality, rate) when doing manufacturing at-scale. Toward this goal, the big part of this class is to design and manufacture our own yoyo. For this project, we went through a process of designing the metal mold, thermoforming parts and actually injection-molding our own yoyos.
