## NASA/Aerodyne 

- My work at NASA GSFC / Aerodyne is primarily with the Roman Space Telescope's Integrated Modelling Team (RST IM), where I assist with mechanical jitter testing and analysis for RST, as well as developing internal tools support our analysis process. There are great cares that go into to making sure mechanical jitters and thermal distortion effects are well-managed and understood, all with the goal of ensuring sufficient optical performance for the space telescope. Here are some of the publicly available publications that give an overview into what this process looks like: 

#### Systems Design and Analysis of the Roman Space Telescope 
K. -C. A. Liu et al., "Achieving Stability – Systems Design and Analysis of the Roman Space Telescope," 2025 IEEE Aerospace Conference, Big Sky, MT, USA, 2025, pp. 1-17, doi: 10.1109/AERO63441.2025.11068696.

#### Measuring and Mitigating Roman Space Telescope Reaction Wheel Imbalance Forces and Torques 
P. M. Lin, L. Sokolsky, R. B. Campion and K. -C. A. Liu, "Measuring and Mitigating Roman Space Telescope Reaction Wheel Imbalance Forces and Torques," 2025 IEEE Aerospace Conference, Big Sky, MT, USA, 2025, pp. 1-9, doi: 10.1109/AERO63441.2025.11068567.
