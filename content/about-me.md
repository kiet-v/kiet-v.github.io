I’m Kiet Vu, currently a Master’s student at MIT building an ultrasound kit gearing toward educational purpose. My interests are in electronics, controls & instrumentation, and design of electromechanical systems. My dream is to create new inventions, and toward that goal I am trying to learn and acquire new skills so I can expand my toolkits and be able to draw ideas from many different sources. 
<br/><br/>
I was also an undergraduate at MIT in the Mechanical Engineering department (Class of 2023). Before starting my master program, I worked for a year in the system engineering role at NASA GSFC as a contractor for the Roman Space Telescope project, performing and assisting with mechanical jitter and thermal distortion analysis.
